Red Pill Diaries Publisher

A small publishing tool for the animated series The Red Pill Diaries.

The Red Pill Diaries Publisher is used by the creator of The Red Pill Diaries to upload finished, creator-approved episodes of the series to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, alongside the series' YouTube releases.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok and authorizes the tool (Login Kit).
  2. Before anything is posted, the tool shows which TikTok account will receive the post and the privacy options that account allows.
  3. Only videos the creator has explicitly approved are queued. At the scheduled time the tool uploads the video with the creator's caption and chosen privacy level.
  4. The tool checks the post's processing status and records the result.

The tool doesn't post for anyone else, doesn't read other users' content, and doesn't sell or share data.
