Privacy Policy

AI Content Publisher

1. Summary

AI Content Publisher (the "Service") is a personal tool that its operator uses to post the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account. It collects only what TikTok's APIs need to do that.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Your TikTok open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar), through the user.info.basic scope.
  • An access token and refresh token, which let the Service post on the operator's behalf through the video.publish and video.upload scopes.
  • Creator information that TikTok returns before posting (nickname, allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length), and the status of each post.

3. How it's used

Only to show which account will receive a post, to upload the operator's approved videos, and to check whether each post succeeded. It isn't used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and post records are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator. Nothing is stored on this website, which is a static page with no cookies, trackers or analytics.

5. Sharing

Data isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone. The only party that receives anything is TikTok, which receives the videos and captions being posted.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked; post records are kept as a publishing log. To revoke access, go to TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions) and remove the Service. The operator then deletes the stored tokens.

7. Children

The Service isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date below shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/lumpy0326-crypto/lumpy0326-crypto.github.io/issues.
